No. AngebotPilot is a tool for creating and managing quotations. Can I write invoices with AngebotPilot â€“ including e-invoices?

Yes. Invoices are created with one click from a job or an accepted quotation, or you write them from scratch. When issued, an invoice receives the next consecutive number and is locked; corrections are made with a cancellation invoice. The PDF contains the structured invoice data in ZUGFeRD format (profile EN 16931) â€“ so it meets the requirements for an e-invoice between businesses in Germany. You can also download the XML file separately. Payments, part payments and payment reminders are managed right on the invoice. AngebotPilot is currently not designed for invoices to public-sector clients that require the XRechnung format. If in doubt, check mandatory details and tax treatment with your tax adviser.

Is online acceptance an electronic signature?

No, it is not a qualified electronic signature within the meaning of the eIDAS Regulation. Via their personal link, your customer makes a documented declaration: AngebotPilot stores the name they enter, the time and an optional message. This is a traceable record of the acceptance. Where the law requires a particular form (for example the written form), online acceptance does not replace it.
