Can I change a quotation after sending it?

Yes. Any changes made after sending are saved as a new version of the quotation, so there is always a clear record of what your customer received.

How do I stop reminders?

Reminders stop automatically as soon as your customer makes a decision or unsubscribes. You set the timing in your company settings, where you can also switch reminders off.

Is online acceptance legally binding?

Online acceptance is a documented declaration by your customer, recording their name and the time, but it is not a qualified electronic signature. Where a specific legal form is required by law, it does not replace that form. AngebotPilot does not provide legal advice.
